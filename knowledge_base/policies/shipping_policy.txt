# NovaTech Electronics - Shipping and Delivery Policy
[FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE]

1. Domestic Shipping Options (United States)
NovaTech ships to all 50 US states, Washington D.C., Puerto Rico, and APO/FPO military addresses. The following shipping options are available at checkout:

a) Standard Shipping:
   - Delivery Time: 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.
   - Cost: $4.99 flat rate per order.
   - Free Standard Shipping: Available automatically on all orders totaling $50.00 or more before taxes and fees. No coupon code required.

b) Express 2-Day Shipping:
   - Delivery Time: Guaranteed delivery within 2 business days after dispatch.
   - Cost: $14.99 flat rate per order.
   - Availability: Orders must be placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) on business days. Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST will be dispatched the next business day, adding 1 extra day.
   - Available for all contiguous 48 US states only. Not available for Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, or APO/FPO addresses.

c) Overnight Priority Delivery:
   - Delivery Time: Guaranteed delivery on the next business day after dispatch.
   - Cost: $24.99 flat rate per order.
   - Availability: Orders must be placed Monday through Thursday before 1:00 PM EST. Orders placed on Friday after 1:00 PM EST will not be dispatched until Monday.
   - Not available for weekends, federal holidays, Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, or APO/FPO addresses.

2. Order Processing & Dispatch Times
- Orders placed Monday through Friday before 2:00 PM EST: Processed and dispatched from the warehouse on the same business day.
- Orders placed Monday through Friday after 2:00 PM EST: Processed and dispatched the following business day.
- Orders placed on Saturday or Sunday: Processed and dispatched on the following Monday (or next business day if Monday is a federal holiday).
- Orders placed on US federal holidays: Processed and dispatched on the next business day.

3. International Shipping
NovaTech currently ships internationally to the following countries and regions only: Canada, United Kingdom, European Union member states, Australia, and Japan. International shipping is not available to other countries at this time.

International Shipping Options:
a) International Standard Shipping:
   - Delivery Time: 7 to 14 business days after dispatch (varies by destination country and customs clearance).
   - Cost: $19.99 flat rate per order.

b) International Express Shipping:
   - Delivery Time: 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.
   - Cost: $39.99 flat rate per order.

Customs & Import Duties:
- NovaTech ships all international orders on a DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) basis.
- All customs duties, import taxes, and Value Added Tax (VAT) are calculated and collected at checkout.
- The customer will NOT receive any unexpected customs fees or import charges upon delivery. What you pay at checkout is the final total.

4. Package Tracking
- A tracking number is automatically generated and emailed to the customer's registered email address once the order departs the NovaTech warehouse.
- Tracking is available in real-time through the carrier's website (FedEx, UPS, or USPS depending on the shipping method selected).
- Tracking information may take up to 12 hours to update after dispatch.
- Customers can also view their tracking status by logging into their NovaTech account under "Order History."

5. Signature Confirmation Requirement
- Orders with a total value exceeding $500.00 USD require an adult signature upon delivery.
- Products requiring signature include: NovaBook Pro 15 laptops ($1,499.00), NovaPhone X12 smartphones ($799.00 and above), and any order whose cart total exceeds $500.00.
- If no one is available to sign, the carrier will leave a delivery notice and attempt redelivery up to 2 more times before returning the package to the local carrier facility for customer pickup.

6. Damaged Packages Upon Delivery
- If a package arrives visibly damaged (crushed box, torn packaging, visible cracks), the customer must:
  Step 1: Take clear photographic evidence of the damaged packaging and product before opening further.
  Step 2: Report the damage to NovaTech Support within 48 hours of the delivery timestamp.
  Step 3: NovaTech will file a carrier claim on the customer's behalf and provide a prepaid return shipping label.
  Step 4: The customer will receive either a full refund or a free replacement unit — customer's choice.
- Damage reported after 48 hours of delivery may not be eligible for a transit damage claim and may instead be processed as a warranty or return case.

7. Lost or Missing Packages
- If the tracking status shows "Delivered" but the package cannot be located:
  Step 1: Wait 24 hours. Carriers sometimes scan packages as "Delivered" up to 24 hours before actual delivery.
  Step 2: Check all delivery locations around your address (porch, mailbox, neighbor's porch, building reception desk).
  Step 3: If still not found after 24 hours, contact NovaTech Support to open a formal carrier claim investigation.
  Step 4: Carrier investigations typically take 3 to 5 business days. NovaTech will notify you of the outcome.
  Step 5: If the carrier confirms the package is lost, NovaTech will ship a replacement at no additional charge.
