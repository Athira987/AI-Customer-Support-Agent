# NovaTech Electronics - Return and Refund Policy
[FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE]

1. Standard Return Window
NovaTech Electronics offers a 30-day standard return window for most hardware products starting from the exact date the shipment is delivered to the customer's address.

2. Return Eligibility & Condition
To qualify for a full refund:
- Items must be returned in their original packaging with all included accessories, documentation, power adapters, and cables.
- The item must show no signs of physical abuse, water ingress, scratches, or cosmetic damage.
- Customer must perform a factory reset and remove all personal accounts (Google Account, Apple ID, Windows PIN/Passwords) before returning smart devices.

3. Restocking Fees & Deductions
- Unopened items returned within 30 days: 100% full refund with $0 restocking fee.
- Opened, non-defective items in working condition: Subject to a 10% restocking fee to cover inspection and repackaging.
- Items missing original components or packaging: Subject to a deduction of up to 25% of the purchase price or rejection of the return.

4. Non-Returnable Items (Final Sale)
The following items cannot be returned or refunded once purchased:
- Downloadable software licenses, digital gift cards, and extended warranty service plans.
- In-ear earbuds (NovaBuds) once the hygiene security seal has been broken, unless proven defective upon arrival.
- Customized or engraved devices.

5. Return Process & Shipping Labels
- To initiate a return, customers must request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number through their online NovaTech account or by contacting support.
- NovaTech provides a prepaid return shipping label for items that are defective on arrival (DOA) or damaged in transit.
- For buyer's remorse returns (e.g., changed mind or wrong color chosen), the customer is responsible for return shipping costs ($8.99 flat deduction if using our prepaid label).

6. Refund Timelines
- Once the returned item is received at our logistics warehouse, inspection takes 2 to 4 business days.
- Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days depending on the customer's financial institution.
